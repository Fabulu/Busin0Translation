## Capacity Constraints

Japanese katakana/kanji encode as 1 glyph each, but English letters also
encode as 1 glyph each. The constraint is the fixed slot count per entry.
Many English translations are truncated to fit. This is the expected behavior
for in-place patching -- the alternative (resizing entries) would require
rebuilding the offset table and all pointer references, which is fragile.

Key truncation hotspots:
- Spell names: 4-6 glyph slots (very tight)
- Spell descriptions: 10-39 slots (truncation common)
- Quest descriptions: 31-102 slots (major truncation)
- Allied action descriptions: 82-86 slots (tight but workable)
- Equipment categories: 2-10 slots (heavy truncation)
